ESP32 Wi‑Fi setup

Scan for nearby networks, select your 2.4 GHz home Wi‑Fi, then enter its password if the network is secured. Open networks are labeled.

Changing networks temporarily disconnects the house connector. If the router assigns a new ESP32 address, update ESP32_URL in the connector environment and restart it.

Hidden networks can be entered by typing their SSID. First-time or recovery setup: connect locally to SmartHouse-Setup, then open the ESP32 Wi‑Fi setup page.
